# Privacy Request Procedure

November 13, 2026. Operational draft for A-06; counsel must confirm applicable
rights, exceptions and deadlines before activation.

## Intake And Identity

Contact: **accounts@toinky.com**. Accountable operator: **Toinky**; coordinator:
Randi, the accountable owner/Super Admin. Confirm the mailbox is monitored and
appoint a substitute before launch. Log a restricted request ID, date received,
request type, scope, assigned owner and response deadline. Acknowledge promptly.
Do not put requests, identity documents, exports or passwords in public issues.

Verify identity proportionately, preferably through an authenticated account
and a verified email. Never ask the requester to disclose a password, token or
payment-card details. Disabled/deleted accounts and people described inside a
story need a separate verification path; matching a public display name is not
enough. Avoid collecting identity documents unless necessary, and set a deletion
date if they are used. Consider an authorized representative or guardian under
the applicable law, while protecting the other person's information.

## Scope And Response

My Profile supplies current-password-confirmed JSON export, display-name/photo
correction, verified email change, consent withdrawal and identity deletion.
The export deliberately excludes credentials, other players' private character
information and raw mixed-person AI context. It is a self-service starting point,
not the full answer to every statutory access request.

Search the inventory for relevant account IDs, current/former addresses within
their ownership dates, authored work, game membership, review/audit notes,
AI text, media, provider records and operational logs. Historical addresses
before migration 144 may not be recoverable automatically: document gaps and
investigate proportionately. Redact other people's personal/private material;
explain withheld categories and the applicable reason. A large export must be
delivered securely, with a short-lived authorized retrieval process if email
would expose information. Log delivery without retaining a second indefinite
copy of the full export.

Corrections must reach the source record and relevant published derivatives or
recipients where required. Explain a disputed fact or inability to alter a
pinned history. Do not silently rewrite another player's fictional outcome.

The owner chose to retain **shared fiction, shared media and game history with
account identity removed**. Identity deletion removes the user row, profile
photo, credentials/codes, consent/address history, friendships, invitations,
ratings/review text and direct attribution. Shared game hosts/authors are
detached or assigned the common disabled “Deleted player” identity. Account
purchase-provider references and free-text ledger notes are cleared; financial
amounts remain. This is not proof of irreversible anonymization of free text.

Personal information embedded in story prose, images, snapshots, review
packages or other shared text requires a privacy request. Assess a lawful
redaction/removal route, identify copies/derivatives and providers, protect
other players, and record the result. Preserve only information justified by a
specific applicable exception; tell the requester what remains and why. Do not
equate detaching a database foreign key with deleting all personal content.

Consent withdrawal disables use and revokes existing sessions. Under the
proposed policy, accounts that accepted that policy are eligible for identity
erasure after 365 disabled days. Older unconsented accounts are excluded from
automatic erasure until there is an appropriate notice/basis. A person can ask
for earlier erasure. A Super Admin request requires ownership transfer, which
the operator must arrange rather than treating the role as an exemption.

## Deadlines And Escalation

Use the applicable jurisdiction and request type, not a blanket universal
deadline. For planning, triage within two business days and use the shortest
applicable legal deadline once established; this is an internal target.

- PIPEDA access is generally answered within **30 days**, with limited lawful
  extensions and notice requirements: [OPC access principle](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/privacy-laws-in-canada/the-personal-information-protection-and-electronic-documents-act-pipeda/p_principle/principles/p_access/).
- Alberta PIPA access generally has a **45-day** response period, subject to
  applicable extension rules: [Alberta OIPC access requests](https://oipc.ab.ca/how-to-make-an-access-request/).
- GDPR requests generally require a response within **one month**; permitted
  extensions require timely explanation: [European Commission individual rights](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/information-individuals_en).

Escalate uncertainty, proposed refusal, fees, identity disputes, deadline risk,
children's information and legal holds to the owner/privacy adviser promptly.
Record the basis for any extension/refusal and the available complaint route.

## Completion And Backups

Check the live result with authorized, minimal queries or fixture-equivalent
checks. Track provider action separately: Stripe, hosting, email and gateway
copies are not automatically erased by deleting Toinky's row. Confirm backup
retention and restoration controls with the host; do not promise an unverified
backup expiry. Apply a restricted erasure ledger before restoring old backups
so erased identities are not silently reactivated. Such an operational ledger
must be minimal, access-controlled and have its own reviewed retention period;
the application does not yet automate this restoration step.

Send a plain-language completion response describing completed actions,
remaining copies/grounds, any redactions and escalation rights. Close the
restricted request log with date, decider and evidence reference. Set reviewed
retention/deletion dates for the request record and supporting verification.
