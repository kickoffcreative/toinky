# Throne Tomas Late Decision Rollback

Before this batch, the studio has no fixed CORONATION_HOUR / TOMAS decision.
Existing Tomas participation consists of SEALED_ARCHIVE plus conditional
opportunities. None of those templates, guards or effects are removed.

Add one active Person Playing This Character decision at CORONATION_HOUR,
with a 90-second window and three options:

| Code | Consequences |
|---|---|
| REQUIRE_WRITTEN_ORDERS | COURT_TRUST +4, STABILITY -2 |
| KEEP_FORCE_NEUTRAL | UNREST -4, LEGITIMACY -2 |
| SERVE_COUNCIL_ORDER | STABILITY +5, COURT_TRUST -3 |

All consequences are generic FACTOR effects. No option sets a claimant,
letter holder, review result, security allegiance flag, or crown holder.
The scene concerns how the captain accepts orders, including when the crown
is already occupied. It does not declare an attack or invent a legal judgment.

To roll back, deactivate this new decision and its three new options through
the studio, or restore the preceding published revision. Do not delete older
Tomas opportunities or rewrite completed games. Fresh-version gameplay remains
required, including cases with and without a completed coronation.
