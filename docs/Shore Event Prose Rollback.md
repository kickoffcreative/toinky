## EVENT_STORM — Player-Facing Prose

The yacht is caught in a violent storm, its hull battered by nature's wrath. The wind howls through the trees, and waves crash against the jagged rocks with a deafening roar. Lightning flashes across the sky, illuminating the dark, mist-shrouded island and casting eerie shadows over the survivors. The storm intensifies, tearing through the ship with relentless force, until the vessel is finally torn apart, leaving the survivors stranded on the island's jagged north shore.

The air is thick with salt and the scent of rain, while the sound of the storm drowns out all other noises, forcing the survivors to face their new reality, now entangled with the island's shroud.

As the storm rages on, the survivors cling to the remnants of the ship, their hearts pounding with fear and uncertainty. The ocean, once a source of freedom, now feels like a prison, and the island, once an unknown destination, has become a place of both danger and mystery.

With the storm finally subsiding, the four survivors are left to confront the reality of their situation: they are stranded on an unknown shore. With their hopes more bruised than they were on the outside, they gather what they can from the wreckage, and build shelter on the shores of this stranger's land.

## EVENT_STORM — Recap Transition

They had sailed out laughing, four friends on a borrowed yacht, someone filming the approach — and then the sky closed. The storm took the yacht, and the shore took them. By the time the wind dropped they were four survivors on a jagged north shore, gathering what the sea had left them and building shelter out of wreckage.
