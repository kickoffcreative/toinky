## Aftermath — Section Order 10, Priority 100

Unconditional.

What the island keeps: one yacht, settling deeper into the Fangs with every tide. One fire-pit, swept. One charm, re-hung. What the survivors carry home is less tidy: a father's name restored to the present tense or laid finally to rest; a mother's thirteen years, which no apology can refund; a brother, a captain, a friend — present, or present only in the telling. Anaise writes it all down, in the end. She changes the names. She does not change the island. On the last page she writes the only sentence the whole story was ever about: some doors are closed because of what is behind them, and some are closed because of what is in front of them, and wisdom is knowing the difference before the tide decides for you. She leaves with the survivors.

## Invitation — Section Order 80, Priority 100

Unconditional.

Imago Island is still there. The charts still lie about the Fangs; the volcano still sleeps its shallow sleep; the door in the Cave of Sorrows still breathes against its stones, patient as ever. Another season is coming, and the sea — generous, careless — is already fitting out another yacht, another crew, another storm. The island does not hunt. It simply waits to be found. Gather your people. The story is ready when you are.

Anaise's old books were right about almost everything, which is the most frightening outcome of all. The island has a memory, a door, and a keeper, and the group leaves knowing all three — knowledge that will sit in their dreams for years, patient as tide.

## Truth's Cost — Section Order 10, Priority 100

They come home with the truth between them like a carried table — heavy, awkward, set down gently in every room it enters for the rest of their lives. What was hidden is known, and knowing did not break them. That is rarer than rescue.

## Truth's Cost — Section Order 30, Priority 100

TRUST LT 30.

The secrets outlast the rescue. Rich man's son, poor man's performance; a mother revealed at the shore; a girlfriend's camera holding it all — the truth came out the way things come out of the Cave of Sorrows: at a price, and not entirely. Some of them will never be in the same room again. The island keeps what it is owed.

## Group's Fate — Section Order 20, Priority 100

SIGNAL_FIRE NOT_IN Spotted; SAFETY GTE 30.

No sail turns. The horizon stays empty, and the group does the hardest arithmetic there is: rations, water, work, hope, divided five ways. They are not rescued. They are, however, ready — a camp kept, a fire kept, each other kept. The island has not beaten them. It has enrolled them. Jerome stays with the group.

The group asks the water for her brother the whole way home. The water, which took Bernard the same way, says nothing. Jerome does not come home with the group.

## Spirit — Section Order 30, Priority 100

VESSEL IN Jerome,Roberto,Anaise,Brianna.

One of them comes home wrong. The family will say the island changed them — the new patience, the smile a half-second slow, the way they stand at windows looking out to sea. Recovery will be attempted. Anniversaries will be observed. And every year, on the storm's date, they will ask to be taken back to the water, and no one will understand why they need to go.

## Brianna's Answer — Section Order 90, Priority 200

Unconditional fallback.

In the end, Brianna does not choose — not the cave, not the shore, not the name her mother carried. She leaves the island with the question still open, the boat moving through water that gives back nothing, and the knowledge that some answers are not taken but waited for.
